Drag empty space to pan; Shift+drag for a marquee (right-to-left also selects what it touches). Click / Shift+click select; drag a node to move it; drag from a port to connect (red cross = refused, pipe and signal ports don't mix). G toggles grid snapping (Alt skips it for one drag). Del delete, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo, arrows nudge, F fit all, wheel zoom. Outside edits: M move pump, E toggle valve error.
