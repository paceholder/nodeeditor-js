Click / Shift+click select; drag empty space for a marquee (right-to-left also selects what it touches); drag a node to move it (Alt skips the grid); drag from a port to connect (red cross = refused, pipe and signal ports don't mix). Del delete, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo, arrows nudge, F fit all, wheel zoom, middle-drag or Space+drag pan. Outside edits: M move pump, E toggle valve error.
