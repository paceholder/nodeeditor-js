Drag empty space to pan; Shift+drag for a marquee (right-to-left also selects what it touches). Click / Shift+click select; drag a node to move it; drag from a port to connect (red cross = refused, pipe and signal ports don't mix). G toggles grid snapping (Alt skips it for one drag). Ctrl/Cmd+C, X, V copy, cut, paste (at the pointer), Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicate. Del delete, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo, arrows nudge, F fit all, wheel zoom. R / Shift+R rotate, M / Shift+M mirror left-right / top-bottom. Select the tank alone and drag its corner handle to resize it (Shift keeps the aspect ratio). Drags near the edge of the view scroll it. Right-click (or long-press, or Shift+F10) opens a context menu. Connect the sensor's "level" to the controller's "setpoint" to pick a link kind; delayed links are drawn orthogonally. "Orthogonal wires" switches the others. Outside edits: P move pump, E toggle valve error.
